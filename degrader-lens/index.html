Working…

Upload a patent PDF and the tool reads every page: compound identifiers, reported IC50 / DC50 / Dmax, and any machine-readable SMILES in tables or the SI (each validated with RDKit). With “read drawn structures too” ticked, it also finds the pages with drawings, has Claude read each one into a SMILES, and merges every structure onto its compound by example number.
